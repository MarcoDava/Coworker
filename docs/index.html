Features

Everything you need to stay accountable

Built for two people who want to work together but aren't in the same room.

🖥️

Live screen streaming

Your friend's real desktop appears on their in-world laptop. Peer-to-peer over WebRTC — no server in the middle.

👁️

Peek mode

Hold Alt to swing the camera to your friend's screen and see exactly what they're working on.

🎯

Automatic scoring

The app checks focused window titles against a blacklist you both agree on. Off-task? Points deducted silently.

🔔

Call out

Catch your friend slacking? Press Space while peeking to call them out for bonus points.

💤

Idle penalty

No keyboard or mouse input for 2+ minutes? You silently lose points. No coasting allowed.

🎮

Discord Rich Presence

Your session status shows up in your Discord profile automatically.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes

No account. No config. Just install and share a code.

1

Download and install

Run the Coworker Setup installer. Windows 10/11 required.

2

One person hosts

Open the app and create a room — you'll get a 6-digit code.

3

Your friend joins

They enter the same code and you're both in the room together.

4

Agree on the rules, start the timer

Set your blacklist in the lobby, lock in, and get to work.
